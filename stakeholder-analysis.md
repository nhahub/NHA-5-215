## Influence and engagement summary

- **Manage closely:** course instructor/evaluator and project leader.
- **Involve in planning and reviews:** sub leader, QA automation engineers, and QA/test analysts.
- **Respect and verify constraints:** Swag Labs / SauceDemo site owner — use only the public demo environment.
- **Represent through scenarios:** e-commerce customers and test users.

## Assumptions and items to confirm

- The team members and roles above come from the current project proposal (Group G1).
- Swag Labs (SauceDemo) is a public demo site intended for automated testing practice.
- The team will not test a production system or use real customer/payment data.
- All checkout data used in tests is synthetic.
